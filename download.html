Before you install.

Is the mobile app really the same library as the website?

Yes. StoryTeller Mobile and the web library share a single backend. A story you publish on one shows up on the other within seconds. Bookmarks, likes, drafts, and reading progress all sync across devices tied to your account.

Do I need an account to read on mobile?

No. Reading stays anonymous everywhere. You only need an account to write, save drafts, bookmark stories, or follow authors — the same rule as on the web.

Are there ads in the mobile app?

A single banner above the tab bar, and occasional native ad cards between stories in the feed. We never place ads mid-paragraph inside a story. When you're offline, no ads show at all.

Can I sideload the APK without Google Play?

Yes. The direct APK download above is signed and identical to the Play Store build. You'll need to enable "Install from unknown sources" once in your Android settings.

What's different between the app and the website?

The app adds offline reading, push notifications, and a lighter compose flow optimized for touch. The website has a richer editor, author analytics, and a broader browse view. Most readers use both.
